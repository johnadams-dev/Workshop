Text Messaging Enrollment

AC Pathfinder Coaching Program — Adams, Cameron & Co. Realtors

What you'll receive

By enrolling, you agree to receive recurring automated SMS text messages from Adams, Cameron & Co. at the phone number you provide, including:

  • Weekly coaching check-in questions
  • Activity reminders and task nudges related to your business plan
  • Open house, listing appointment, and showing preparation reminders
  • Coaching session confirmations and scheduling messages
  • Motivational and accountability messages from your coach

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your coaching plan and activity level. You will typically receive between 1 and 5 messages per week during active coaching periods.

Message and data rates

Standard message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply. Adams, Cameron & Co. does not charge for messages sent through this program.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message from our program number (+1 386-222-0033) at any time, or use the "Unenroll" option below. After opting out, you'll receive one final confirmation message.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, or contact johnadams@adamscameron.com / (386) 299-9504.

Voluntary participation

Enrollment is entirely voluntary. You will not be penalized professionally for choosing not to enroll, and you may opt out at any time without affecting your standing with Adams, Cameron & Co.

Sign in with your @adamscameron.com account to enroll or manage your text messaging preferences.
