Sales Leader
Total dollar volume of every deal that went under contract this month — your own listing, another Adams Cameron agent's listing where you represented the buyer, or a cooperating brokerage's listing where you represented the buyer. Split a deal with a teammate? You're each credited for your share of the commission split; solo agents get full credit. Deals that later fall through drop off the count.

Listing Leader

Co-listed properties split the count itself by your share (a 50/50 co-listing earns each agent 0.5), so totals can land on numbers like "2.5 listings." Ties are broken by combined list price.
