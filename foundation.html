About the Foundation

The Adams Cameron Foundation collects voluntary agent contributions at each real estate closing. Participating agents pledge a fixed dollar amount or a small percentage of their commission — collected by the company and distributed to our partner organizations on a regular basis. There is no minimum commitment and participation can be changed at any time.

  • 🫀
    American Heart AssociationFighting heart disease and stroke through research, education, and advocacy
  • 🎗
    American Cancer SocietyFunding cancer research and providing support to patients and families
  • 🎓
    Darcy Akers ScholarshipSupporting local students pursuing higher education in our community
  • 🏥
    AdventHealth FoundationExpanding access to health and wellness care across our region
