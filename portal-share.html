Every customer you've represented gets a personal page about their home: you front and centre, where their deal stands, listing links and tour views, contract dates, and after closing, review and referral buttons.

Preview shows you exactly what they see. Copy link copies their personal link to text or email them — it's the same link every time. Edit on a deal lets you add a seller or buyer, their email, phone and mailing address, and a nickname to greet them by (e.g. "John" for the Adams Family Trust). Customers without an email still get a link — text it to them. Customers with an email on their deal can also request their own link at johnadams.org/portal.html.
