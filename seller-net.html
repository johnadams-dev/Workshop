Property taxes

Florida taxes are paid in arrears. Before November 1 the seller owes the buyer for the days they owned the home this year; from November 1 the bill is assumed paid and the buyer credits the seller for the rest of the year.

Financing

Closing costs

Lines left blank aren't printed. Mortgage stamps, intangible tax and the lender's title policy are calculated from each loan. The owner's title policy is left to the seller, as on the seller net.

Prepaids, credits and the monthly payment

Prepaid interest runs from closing day through the end of that month. The escrow reserve is that many months of tax and insurance held by the lender.
